About

Who is Annatoria?

Annatoria is recognised as a powerhouse within the music industry. She is a multi-award-winning music artist, songwriter, and influencer. Annatoria's music and voice has graced the stage across the world, where she has performed to thousands within the United Kingdom, USA and Africa.

The Voice UK

How it all started

A defining moment in Annatoria's journey came when she stepped onto the stage of The Voice UK. With her soulful voice and captivating presence, she introduced her artistry to a much wider audience — marking the beginning of a new chapter in her musical journey.
